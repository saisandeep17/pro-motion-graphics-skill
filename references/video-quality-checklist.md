# Video Quality Assurance & Audit Checklist (V2 Production Gate)

Every video generated by an AI agent must be audited against this checklist before delivery. Never mark a video task as complete without passing these gates.

---

## 1. Pre-Flight Visual Inspection (Still Frames)

Render representative still frames at 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of timeline duration (`npx remotion still ... --frame=N`):

- [ ] **Frame 0 Hook Check**: Does the video start in motion on Frame 0, or is there an amateur blank fade-in?
- [ ] **Zero-Slop Color Check**: Is the canvas free of floating Gaussian blur blobs and purple-to-cyan mesh gradients?
- [ ] **Concentric Radii Validation**: Do nested containers follow $R_{inner} = \max(0, R_{outer} - Padding)$?
- [ ] **Contrast Ratio**: Does foreground text pass WCAG AA contrast ($>4.5:1$ against the immediate background layer)?
- [ ] **Hairline Grid Crispness**: Are borders and grid lines rendered as exact 1px solid lines rather than fuzzy blurred lines?

---

## 2. Typography & Spatial Alignment

- [ ] **No Text Clipping**: Are all headings, descenders (g, y, p, q), and ascenders fully visible without bounding box clipping?
- [ ] **Display Tracking**: Does headline display type ($>48\text{px}$) use negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.05em`)?
- [ ] **Monospace Formatting**: Are metadata labels, timestamps, and tags set in uppercase monospace with positive tracking (`+0.06em` to `+0.10em`)?
- [ ] **Tabular Numerics**: Do all animated counters and metric displays declare `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to eliminate horizontal digit jitter?
- [ ] **No Orphan Words**: Does headline copy use `text-wrap: balance` and appropriate line length?

---

## 3. Motion Physics & Choreography

- [ ] **Deterministic Math**: Are all animations strictly driven by a `frame` integer or normalized timeline `progress` ($0 \to 1$)?
- [ ] **No Unseeded Randoms**: Is the codebase completely free of raw `Math.random()`, `setTimeout`, or unanchored CSS `@keyframes`?
- [ ] **Staggered Hierarchy**: Do grouped elements enter with a 3–6 frame offset in natural reading order rather than all at once?
- [ ] **Transition Diversity**: Do consecutive scenes vary spatial momentum (e.g. Scene 1 wipes vertically, Scene 2 pushes in via camera zoom)?
- [ ] **GPU-Safe Properties**: Are 95%+ of animations using `transform`, `opacity`, `clip-path`, or `stroke-dashoffset` instead of animating `width`, `height`, `top`, or `filter: blur()`?

---

## 4. Platform Safe Zones

### Vertical Social (`9:16` / `1080x1920`)
- [ ] **Top Safe Zone**: Header elements clear the top 220px margin (avoids story headers, status bars).
- [ ] **Bottom Safe Zone**: CTA buttons clear the bottom 340px margin (avoids platform caption, like buttons, audio handles).
- [ ] **Side Margins**: Content maintains at least 80px padding from left and right edges.

### Widescreen (`16:9` / `1920x1080`)
- [ ] **Title Safe Area**: All critical copy lies within the central 90% boundary (`96px` horizontal, `54px` vertical padding).

---

## 5. Audio & Synchronization (When Audio is Present)

- [ ] **Voiceover Ducking**: Does the background music duck by 12–16dB during speech segments?
- [ ] **Impact Synchronization**: Do hit SFX align to the exact frame where visual elements make contact?
- [ ] **No Audio Popping**: Do intro and outro audio tracks have at least 15–30 frames of smooth fade-in and fade-out?
- [ ] **Licensing Verification**: Are all music and sound assets authorized for commercial/royalty-free distribution?

---

## 6. Output File Integrity

Use `scripts/inspect-output` to verify:

- [ ] **File Existence**: The rendered file exists on disk and is non-zero in size.
- [ ] **Pixel Dimensions**: Matches target resolution exactly (`1920x1080`, `1080x1920`, etc.).
- [ ] **Frame Rate**: Output file FPS matches configured composition FPS.
- [ ] **Color Space**: Encoded as `yuv420p` for maximum web and mobile playback compatibility.
- [ ] **Audio Stream**: Audio stream exists and is encoded as AAC (when audio was requested).
